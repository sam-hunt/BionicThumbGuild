## Interaction log symbols — `BTG_ThumbsUp`'s `rulesStrings`

Vanilla zh quotes cited names in prose with full-width curly quotes
(`任务"{0}"`). Quote `[INITIATOR_nameDef]` / `[RECIPIENT_nameDef]` in the
seven `rulesStrings` lines the same way. Full-width punctuation applies to
any surrounding prose in these lines; placeholders and any digits stay
ASCII.

When a real generation pass runs, record mod-coined terms (bionic thumb
naming, the interaction's flavour phrasing) and any def-to-vanilla-template
reuse here.
