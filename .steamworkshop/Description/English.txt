Bionic Thumb Guild

[h1]About[/h1]
The Bionic Thumb Guild is proud to present its flagship product: [b]the Bionic Thumb[/b].

"But it's a whole hand," you say. Yes. The Guild knows. They just really, really care about thumbs. Every servo, joint, and actuator in the other four fingers exists to support the thumb's extraordinary polyarthric range of motion. The Guild's engineers insist the name is accurate - the thumb [i]is[/i] the hand, the rest is just scaffolding.
